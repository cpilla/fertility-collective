Fertility
Supporting your fertility journey, mind, body, and beyond.
Trying to conceive can bring hope, questions, uncertainty, and a wide range of emotions. Whether you are just beginning, navigating secondary fertility, or preparing for IUI or IVF, we offer a welcoming space to pause, feel supported, and learn practical tools for greater calm and confidence.

Our fertility offerings combine guided hypnosis, relaxation, visualization, and education to support your emotional wellbeing throughout your journey.

Fertility Relaxation Series
Small group · Three sessions · One hour each

A supportive three-session series where you can step away from the demands of your fertility journey and focus on yourself. Each session combines guided relaxation with practical techniques you can use at home, during appointments, or when the waiting feels especially difficult.

Session One: Creating Calm
Explore the connection between mind and body and learn how guided hypnosis and self-hypnosis can support relaxation. Practice breathing and visualization techniques, experience a guided fertility relaxation, and begin creating a simple daily practice.

Session Two: Working With Worries and Uncertainty
Build on your relaxation practice while exploring the thoughts, fears, and expectations that may feel heavy. Guided hypnosis, reflection, and supportive affirmations offer ways to respond to difficult moments with greater compassion and steadiness.

Session Three: Carrying Your Practice Forward
Bring the tools together and explore how to use them through appointments, treatment, waiting periods, and changing circumstances. Practice self-hypnosis and visualization, and create a manageable plan for continuing your relaxation practice beyond the series.

You will have opportunities to connect with others while sharing only what feels comfortable.

Personalized support with time to talk, ask questions, explore your concerns, and experience guided hypnosis tailored to your needs.

Across three sessions, we work together to understand what you are navigating, identify what would feel most supportive, and develop practices that fit your life. Sessions may focus on managing uncertainty, preparing emotionally for appointments or procedures, strengthening confidence, or making space for your own wellbeing.

Your private program includes:

Why Three Sessions?
Learning relaxation and self-hypnosis takes practice. Three sessions give you time to become familiar with the techniques, try them between visits, ask questions, and build on what feels helpful. The goal is to leave with tools you feel comfortable continuing on your own.


Our services provide educational and emotional support and complement your medical care. They do not replace medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and no specific fertility outcome is guaranteed.

Pregnancy & Birth
Support for where you are now—and what comes next.
Pregnancy and preparing for birth can bring different questions, emotions, and needs. We offer dedicated support for both: space to find greater calm throughout pregnancy, and practical preparation as you approach your baby’s birth.

Pregnancy Relaxation & Support
Finding calm, connection, and confidence from the beginning.
You do not have to wait until you are preparing for birth to receive support.

From the earliest weeks, pregnancy can bring excitement alongside uncertainty. You may find yourself waiting for appointments, worrying between milestones, adjusting to changes in your body, or finding it difficult to settle into the experience—especially after a challenging fertility journey or previous loss.

Our pregnancy sessions offer a gentle space to explore those feelings and develop practices that help you feel more grounded. Through guided hypnosis, breathing, visualization, and reflection, we support you in creating more room for rest, connection, and moments of enjoyment, without pressure to feel positive all the time.

Across three sessions, we focus on:

Session One: Creating a Foundation of Calm
Explore your hopes, concerns, and current experience. Begin learning breathing, relaxation, and self-hypnosis practices that you can return to during everyday moments, appointments, or periods of uncertainty.

Session Two: Connection and Compassion
Make space for your changing emotions and relationship with your body. Guided hypnosis, visualization, and journaling invite connection with yourself and your baby at a pace that feels comfortable.

Session Three: Supporting Yourself Through Pregnancy
Bring your tools together into a manageable daily practice. Explore ways to respond to worries, communicate your needs, and continue caring for your emotional wellbeing as pregnancy progresses.

Birth Preparation & Guided Hypnosis
Preparing your mind and body for labor and birth.
As birth approaches, your focus may shift toward what labor will feel like, how you will cope, and what support you will need.

Our birth preparation sessions combine education, guided hypnosis, and practical comfort techniques to help you feel informed and prepared. You will practice ways to soften tension, steady your breathing, focus your attention, and find rest between moments of effort.

We also make space for your questions and birth preferences, helping you communicate what matters to you while remaining supported if circumstances change.

Across three sessions, we focus on:

Session One: Understanding Birth and Building Confidence
Explore the labor and birth process, your expectations, and any worries you are carrying. Begin guided hypnosis and relaxation practices, and identify questions to discuss with your healthcare team.

Session Two: Practicing Tools for Labor
Practice breathing, visualization, self-hypnosis, and focused relaxation. Explore comfort measures, movement, and positions that may support you during labor, according to your needs and your healthcare team’s guidance.

Session Three: Bringing Your Preparation Together
Create a practical plan for using your tools during birth. Explore your preferences, communication with your care team, and ways a partner or support person can help. Include preparation for different birth experiences so you have supportive practices to draw on when plans change.

These tools can be used alongside your chosen pain relief and medical care, whether your birth involves spontaneous labor, induction, or a cesarean.

Three one-hour sessions · Small group

Through guided hypnosis, visualization, intentional breathing, and self-hypnosis, you will learn practical techniques to help calm the nervous system, release tension, and respond to challenging moments with greater steadiness and self-compassion.

The need for more rest, balance, confidence, and self-compassion
