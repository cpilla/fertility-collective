Pregnancy & Birth

Support for where you are now, and what comes next.

Pregnancy and preparing for birth can bring different questions, emotions, and needs. We offer dedicated support for both: space to find greater calm throughout pregnancy, and practical preparation as you approach your baby's birth.

Focus · Pregnancy

Pregnancy Relaxation & Support

Finding calm, connection, and confidence from the beginning.

You do not have to wait until you are preparing for birth to receive support. From the earliest weeks, pregnancy can bring excitement alongside uncertainty. You may find yourself waiting for appointments, worrying between milestones, adjusting to changes in your body, or finding it difficult to settle into the experience, especially after a challenging fertility journey or previous loss.

Our pregnancy sessions offer a gentle space to explore those feelings and develop practices that help you feel more grounded. Through guided hypnosis, breathing, visualization, and reflection, we support you in creating more room for rest, connection, and moments of enjoyment, without pressure to feel positive all the time.

Across three sessions, we focus on
  1. 01
    Creating a Foundation of CalmExplore your hopes, concerns, and current experience. Begin learning breathing, relaxation, and self-hypnosis practices that you can return to during everyday moments, appointments, or periods of uncertainty.
  2. 02
    Connection and CompassionMake space for your changing emotions and relationship with your body. Guided hypnosis, visualization, and journaling invite connection with yourself and your baby at a pace that feels comfortable.
  3. 03
    Supporting Yourself Through PregnancyBring your tools together into a manageable daily practice. Explore ways to respond to worries, communicate your needs, and continue caring for your emotional wellbeing as pregnancy progresses.
Focus · Birth · Private only

Preparing For Your Birth

Preparing your mind and body for labor and birth.

As birth approaches, your focus may shift toward what labor will feel like, how you will cope, and what support you will need. Our birth preparation sessions combine education, guided hypnosis, and birth preferences guidance to help you feel informed and prepared. You will practice ways to soften tension, steady your breathing, focus your attention, and find rest between moments of effort.

We also make space for your questions, helping you communicate what matters to you while remaining supported if circumstances change.

Across three sessions, we focus on
  1. 01
    Understanding Birth and Building ConfidenceExplore the labor and birth process, your expectations, and any worries you are carrying. Begin guided hypnosis and relaxation practices, and identify questions to discuss with your healthcare team.
  2. 02
    Practicing Visualization for LaborPractice breathing, visualization, self-hypnosis, and focused relaxation that may support you during labor, according to your needs and your unique birth experience.
  3. 03
    Bringing Your Preparation TogetherCreate a practical plan for using your tools during birth. Explore your preferences, communication with your care team, and ways a partner or support person can help. Include preparation for different birth experiences so you have supportive practices to draw on when plans change.

These tools can be used alongside your chosen pain relief and medical care, whether your birth involves spontaneous labor, induction, or a cesarean.

Meet the Heart Behind The Fertility Collective

Hi, I'm Jacqui Pilla

I'm a certified hypnotherapist, fertility coach, childbirth educator, and the founder of The Fertility Collective, a community created to support individuals and couples through fertility, pregnancy, birth, and the journey beyond.

My passion for this work began many years ago when my husband and I navigated our own fertility journey while building our family. That experience inspired me to learn everything I could about fertility, pregnancy, birth, and the powerful connection between the mind and body. What began as a personal journey became a calling to help others feel supported, informed, and empowered during some of life's most meaningful transitions.

After becoming a childbirth educator and doula, I continued my training in hypnosis focused on fertility and developed a private practice dedicated to helping individuals and couples find greater calm, confidence, and resilience. Today, I also work with growing families as a childbirth educator at Stork Ready.

I founded The Fertility Collective to be more than a place for guided hypnosis or coaching. I envisioned a welcoming community where people could find education, compassionate guidance, meaningful connection, and tools grounded in evidence to nurture both emotional and physical wellbeing.

Whether you're trying to conceive naturally, navigating IUI or IVF, preparing for birth, or simply looking for someone to walk beside you during an important season of life, you'll be welcomed with understanding, encouragement, and personalized support.

It would be my privilege to walk alongside you as you navigate your own unique journey.
